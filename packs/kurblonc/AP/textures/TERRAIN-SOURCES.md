# Natural cave stone

`blocks/natural_stone.png` is copied unchanged from the owner-supplied
`forager/Forager/assets/minecraft/textures/block/stone.png` (16×16).

## Natural stone walls — owner correction, 2026-10-29

Natural cave walls use the same wall sheets as built stone walls.
`blocks/natural_stone_wall_front.png` is the owner-supplied 64×19 front sheet
(four 16×19 connection cells, the Delverium stone-wall silhouette recolored into
the natural stone palette), and `blocks/natural_stone_wall_top.png` is the
matching 48×32 compact autotile cap generated by
`scripts/build_natural_stone_wall_top.py`: it keeps the built stone cap's pixels
and alpha exactly, so `expandWallTop` produces the same 47 shapes, and remaps
only its body colors onto the natural stone palette. `kurblanc:cave_rock` and
`kurblanc:natural_stone_face` both reference these two sheets, so a cave wall,
a raised natural stone riser and a built stone wall share one geometry, one
lift and one receiver/lighting path.

The superseded `natural_stone_front.png`, `cave_rock_top.png` and
`cave_rock_front.png` repeated the 16×16 tile into flat sheets and let
`top_mask`/`front_mask` cut the shape at load time. Those flat sheets render as
one featureless surface, so the lit/shadowed receiver mask read as long spiked
bands across the cave walls. No source pixels were stretched or recolored; the
art above replaces that derivation.

## Wooden chest — 2026-10-29

`blocks/chest_closed.png` (16×16) and `blocks/chest_open.png` (16×19) are derived
from the owner-supplied Forager normal chest texture at
`forager/Forager/assets/minecraft/textures/entity/chest/normal.png`, using the
lid-front and base-front crops stacked into one tile with a one-pixel transparent
margin and nearest-neighbor pixels. Forager is by CanineGray, licensed
CC BY-NC 4.0; see `LICENSE.txt`. The item icon `../items/chest.png` uses the same
closed silhouette.
